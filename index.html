10DLC Fasttrack — $299 One-Time (Mandatory)

Required for every new business. For upsells, this fee is only applicable if a new 10DLC campaign needs to be added. Existing customers adding seats or numbers to an existing campaign do not require this fee.

These Are Passthrough Fees

All A2P 10DLC charges are passed through at exact cost from TCR, T-Mobile, and carriers. 360 SMS App does not mark up these fees.
